Ledger privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Ledger is a personal tool used only by its owner. This policy describes how it handles Google user data.

What it accesses

Ledger requests the gmail.readonly scope and reads only messages in one label chosen by the owner, which holds investment research emails. It cannot send, delete, modify or label mail.

How the data is used and stored

Message text and attachments are stored on the owner's own computer, used to prepare the owner's private research briefing, and not used for any other purpose. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. A briefing generated from them may be sent to the owner's own Telegram account.

Retention and removal

Data stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Ledger's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
